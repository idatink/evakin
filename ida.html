Our stories

Every doctor saw a dot. No one drew the line.

I
IdaFounder of EVAKIN · caring for her mum since 2011

My mum is 81 and lives with Parkinson's. Caring for her, mostly from London, has been one of the hardest things our family has done. This is the story of why I built EVAKIN.

Every doctor saw a dot

What we needed most was understanding. Each medical provider saw one part of Mum's story, and none of them joined the dots into a line.

One doctor would say, “This should be seen by a gastroenterologist.” The next would say, “This is one for the neurologist.” Nobody could see the whole picture. But the body doesn't work in departments, especially in a complex case like hers.

Eight hospital stays

Mum was admitted to hospital eight times with what looked like her Parkinson's getting worse, including confusion and frightening symptoms. Each time she had antibiotics and IV fluids, got better for a while, and then went downhill again.

Putting everything in one place

Four years ago, when AI came into our lives, I started gathering everything about Mum into one place: every letter, result and note. I researched for months and used AI to educate myself, then my dad and her carer.

That is when the pattern became clear. Her worst episodes weren't her Parkinson's. They were urinary infections, which are more common in older women after menopause and can cause sudden confusion. Like many women of her generation, Mum had never had HRT. Now regular blood and urine tests are part of her routine, and we catch infections early.

The body doesn't work in departments. In a complex story, everything is connected, and someone has to see the whole line.

Even with everything, we needed one system

We are lucky. Mum has a full-time carer at home and several private doctors, and we can afford that. Even so, we needed a system that connected everything, helped us avoid mistakes and reminded us what needed doing.

  • Every new carer needed proper education about Mum, from day one.
  • Every doctor needed the full story, not one chapter.
  • Mum needed to understand herself too: her sleep, her nutrition, her daily rhythms and her care.

Mum talks to EVA too

EVA isn't only for us. Mum talks to EVA to understand what is happening, to ask questions, and sometimes just to talk when her mood is low.

I also made simple movement games for her on a large laptop screen, so she can move a little and enjoy it. That idea grew into the exercise mirror.

Why EVAKIN exists

We learned so much once everything was in one place. EVAKIN is the system I wish we'd had from the start: one place that connects the whole story, for Mum, for us, and for every family caring for someone at home.
